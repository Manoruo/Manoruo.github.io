abstract: >-
  We learn a control policy that intercepts an intruder using only 3D directional unit vectors from a passive
  monocular camera &mdash; without assuming access to relative position or distance. Using analytical policy
  gradients through differentiable quadrotor dynamics, our approach achieves agile interception at speeds up
  to 10&nbsp;m/s, roughly a 30% improvement over point-mass baselines.
