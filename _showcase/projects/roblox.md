<p class="mb-3">
  I was contracted by a <strong>10-million-subscriber YouTube creator</strong>
  to develop a fast-paced multiplayer FPS in Roblox Studio. I initially built
  the project as the sole developer, implementing the game's core systems,
  including weapons, combat mechanics, visual effects, HUDs, progression, and
  shop/inventory systems. As development progressed, I expanded the team to
  include additional contributors for content creation while continuing to lead
  development. The game reached over
  <strong>500,000 plays in under two months</strong> and was nominated for
  <strong>Best Upcoming Experience</strong> at the Roblox Developers
  Conference (RDC).
</p>
